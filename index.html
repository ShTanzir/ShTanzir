01 About

A developer who treats the interface as the product.

I build for real people on real connections — mid-range phones, slow networks, keyboard-only navigation, ultrawide monitors. Pretty is part of it; usable is the requirement.

I’m Sh. Tanzir — a web developer, app developer and creative technologist. My work sits at the intersection of three things that too often get separated: engineering that holds up, design that communicates, and technology that feels alive without getting in the way.

I started where most developers start: breaking markup, restyling components, reading other people’s source. That turned into a habit of building the whole thing — interface, logic, data, deployment — so nothing gets lost between the designer’s file and the browser’s reality.

Today I focus on three lanes. Websites that load fast, rank clearly and are easy for a client to maintain. Apps built with Flutter and Kotlin that respect offline state, permissions and battery. And interactive 3D web moments with Three.js — used deliberately, as storytelling, never as decoration that costs a user their data plan.

The problems I like most: a business with a good offer and a confusing site; a product idea that needs a first working version, not a 40-slide plan; a design that looks great in the mockup and falls apart on a 360px screen. I take those, make them clear, and ship them properly.
